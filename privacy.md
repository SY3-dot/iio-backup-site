# IIO Backup — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: November 3, 2026**

## Purpose

IIO Backup is a private utility used to back up and restore data belonging to the Investment Intelligence OS project. It is not a public application.

## Google Drive Access

IIO Backup uses Google Drive authorization to create, access, and manage backup files associated with the application.

It does not intentionally access unrelated personal files stored in Google Drive.

## Data Storage and Security

Database backups are encrypted locally using rclone Crypt before being uploaded to Google Drive.

Google OAuth credentials and encryption keys are stored on the operator's systems and are not published on this website.

## Data Sharing

The application does not sell or share Google user data with advertisers or third parties. Google Drive is used as the storage provider for encrypted backups.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Backups are retained for disaster recovery and may be deleted by the operator. Automated retention policies may be introduced as the system develops.

The operator can revoke access through Google Account settings and remove the application's backup files from Google Drive.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application operator through the GitHub repository associated with this website.
